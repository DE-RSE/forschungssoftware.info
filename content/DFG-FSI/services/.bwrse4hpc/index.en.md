## Target audience

All researchers in Baden-Württemberg that use HPC systems.

## Service description

> Our team supports software driven research.
> While software is a major contributing factor in modern science, not every domain researcher has the time or expertise to build the best software solution for their problem.
> We alleviate this issue, by providing software development services.
> Our focus is the development of software that runs on HPC clusters, but our services are valuable to both HPC and (currently) non-HPC users.
> Enabling code from non-HPC users to run on HPC systems accelerates their science, and opens up new research directions.
> For HPC users our services can result in more stable, reliable, and faster code.

> We develop your code, either through free of charge mid term (< 6 months) support, or through long term collaboration!
> For more information and contact information, please visit [the bwRSE4HPC website](https://bwrse4hpc.de).

## Status

Operational, funded by the MWK Baden-Württemberg for the period 2025-2027.
