## Project description
> With find.software (Foundations for Interdisciplinary Discovery of (Research) Software), we aim to develop a single point of entry for searching for research software. Imagine you are looking for scientific software, regardless of your scientific field, the programming language or operating system you use, your technical knowledge, or whether you are a developer or a simple user. To this end, we are working together with Wikidata and the scientific community to build a solution for the community and with the community.
